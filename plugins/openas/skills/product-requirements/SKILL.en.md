# Product briefs and requirements

Read research, user decisions and project constraints. Choose a short brief or PRD appropriate to collaboration, retaining requested format/path. Thin inputs may produce an assumption-marked draft; ask only scope-critical gaps.

Define users, problem, value, goals/non-goals and core/error/boundary flows. Give stable requirement IDs with trigger, input/precondition, expected behavior and checkable completion. Avoid unverifiable labels such as high performance or user friendly. Separate confirmed requirements, research inferences and recommendations with sources.

Quality requirements need scenarios, metrics, thresholds and measurement. Unsupported numbers remain proposed targets, not achieved results. Separate product success metrics from engineering completion: include baseline, target, measurement and observation window, marking unknowns pending. Define MVP and exclusions by value/dependencies; use [roadmap planning](../roadmap-planning/SKILL.en.md) for phases.

Deliver background/sources, users/flows, functional/quality requirements, scope/priorities, constraints/dependencies, acceptance, assumptions and open items. Resolve conflicts using original sources and latest user decisions, not silent compromise. Preserve specified technology as a constraint; delegate other choices to [technology selection](../technology-selection/SKILL.en.md). Track changed requirement IDs and downstream effects. A draft is not approval.
