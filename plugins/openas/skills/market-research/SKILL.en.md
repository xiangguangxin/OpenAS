# Market and user research

Start from the specified market, region, users and scenario. Reuse context and ask only gaps changing research direction. Code is not required. Define the supported decision, boundaries, time and resource limits.

Distinguish users, buyers and decision makers; describe current workflows, pain, frequency and switching cost. Separate interviews/behavioral data, public feedback, vendor claims and inference. Public complaints are not representative demand samples.

Verify current information with available tools, recording sources, publication/access dates, geography, samples and definitions. Without network access analyze supplied evidence and disclose freshness gaps. Use [technical research](../technical-research/SKILL.en.md) for source handling and conflict resolution. TAM/SAM/SOM estimates need definitions, units, calculations, ranges and sensitive assumptions; keep unsupported quantities unknown and do not mix years, regions, revenue and user counts or invent interviews/share/willingness to pay.

Use [competitive analysis](../competitive-analysis/SKILL.en.md) to identify fulfilled needs and evidenced gaps. Separate attractiveness from team accessibility, considering acquisition, migration, deployment and buying constraints.

Deliver scope, segments/tasks, demand evidence, market/alternatives, opportunities and counterevidence, confidence, validation methods and stop/pivot conditions. Hypotheses must be falsifiable with bounded interviews, prototypes or trials. Contact and paid trials require authorization. Handoff to [product requirements](../product-requirements/SKILL.en.md) carries evidenced goals and hypotheses, not automatically approved features.
